96 uncharacterised DNA loci are provided. These loci were pulled from a large sequence collection because each encodes a member of one related group of reverse transcriptases (RTs); nothing else about them is known. Characterise this RT and predict its function: report every feature you judge important, the evidence for it, how confident you are, and what experiment would test your main prediction.

The loci are GenBank files in /workdir/data/loci/ (96 files; ORF calls and Pfam hits included). You have bash with prodigal/pyrodigal, HMMER (Pfam-A), BLAST+ and MMseqs2 (a curated protein database; makeblastdb for your own), EMBOSS, mafft, FastTree, ViennaRNA, and Python (biopython, pyhmmer, numpy, scipy, pandas, networkx, matplotlib). No network.

Work on your own and finish by calling `submit_findings`; text that is not submitted through that call is not scored. You have a budget of 1,000,000 output tokens (enforced) and up to 15 hours; if either runs out you will be asked to submit what you have. Each tool call is limited to 10 minutes.
